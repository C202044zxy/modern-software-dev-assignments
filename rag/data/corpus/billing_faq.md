# Billing FAQ

## When is my card charged?
Cards on file are charged on the first of each month for the previous month's
usage. You will receive an emailed invoice 24 hours before the charge.

## What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. ACH bank transfers are
available for accounts on the Enterprise plan.

## How do I update my billing email?
Billing emails are managed separately from user emails. Update the billing
email under Settings > Billing in the dashboard. Invoices will be sent to this
address starting from the next billing cycle.

## Why was my card declined?
The most common reasons are expired cards, insufficient funds, and fraud
protection by the issuing bank. Update your card under Settings > Billing and
the system will retry the charge within one hour.
