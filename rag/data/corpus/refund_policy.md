# Refund Policy

Refunds are available within 30 days of the original charge. Charges older than
30 days are not eligible for refund under any circumstances.

To request a refund, email billing@acme.example with your invoice number and
the reason for the request. Refunds are typically processed within 5 business
days and credited back to the original payment method.

Partial refunds are available for unused portions of annual plans, prorated to
the nearest full month. Monthly plans are not eligible for partial refunds.

Refunds are not available for usage-based charges (such as API call overages)
once the usage has occurred.
